When you connect your TikTok account, we receive your TikTok display name and an access token authorized by you. We do not collect your password.

Access tokens are stored securely and used only for the features above. You can disconnect your account at any time, and we will delete your tokens on request.

Your rights

You can revoke access in your TikTok settings under Security and permissions, or contact us to delete your data.
